# MetaCentrum Account

Read this reference for registration, password changes, expiration, and renewal.
Verify time-sensitive policy against current official documentation.

## Eligibility

- Use is free for research or education.
- Eligible users are members, students, and employees of Czech academic or
  non-commercial research institutions.
- Registration requires a valid academic affiliation.
- Foreign colleagues and collaborators without a valid affiliation must contact
  user support before applying.

## First registration

1. Complete the registration form and its instructions.
2. Check the application-status page if needed.
3. Allow a few working days for manual approval.

The source URLs for the registration, status, and account-management pages were
not supplied. Use current official MetaCentrum links.

## Password change or reset

1. Open **Authentication** in the user profile.
2. Select **Change password**.
3. Change or reset the **einfra password**.

Do not request or expose passwords, recovery secrets, or tokens.

## Annual renewal and expiration

Accounts expire annually on February 2. Renewal reminders are sent around late
December or early January. Renewal requires an application and short activity
summary.

If an account is not renewed by February 2:

- The account expires and becomes hidden.
- Login details and data remain during a three-year grace period.
- A renewal application can reactivate the account during the grace period.
- After three years, the account and associated data are permanently deleted
  without notification.
